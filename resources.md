# 🎮 Trading Simulator Cheat Sheet (Kid-Friendly Edition)

A quick guide to trading toy cards and saving your game coins!

---

## 🧸 1. The Game Words
* **Card (Stock):** A special toy or sticker you buy[cite: 1, 2].
* **Market:** The playground where everyone meets to trade[cite: 1, 2].
* **Bull:** A happy player who thinks prices will go up like a balloon[cite: 1, 2].
* **Bear:** A grumpy player who thinks prices will fall down like a rock[cite: 1, 2].
* **The Spread:** The tiny difference between what a buyer wants to pay and what a seller wants to get[cite: 1, 2].

---

## 🕹️ 2. The 4 Action Buttons

| Button | What It Does | Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Buy Now** *(Market Order)*[cite: 1, 2] | Grabs the card immediately, whatever it costs right now[cite: 1, 2]. | *"I want that shiny card right this second!"*[cite: 1, 2] |
| **Wait for My Price** *(Limit Order)*[cite: 1, 2] | Buys only if the seller drops the price to your lucky number[cite: 1, 2]. | *"I will only pay 5 coins, not 6."*[cite: 1, 2] |
| **Safety Net** *(Stop-Loss)*[cite: 1, 2] | Sells automatically if the price starts dropping so you don't lose all your coins[cite: 1, 2]. | *"If the price falls to 4 coins, sell it so I keep the rest."*[cite: 1, 2] |
| **Pocket the Prize** *(Take-Profit)*[cite: 1, 2] | Sells automatically once you make a good reward[cite: 1, 2]. | *"If it hits 10 coins, sell it and keep the prize!"*[cite: 1, 2] |

---

## 🕯️ 3. Candlesticks (The Mood Bars)

* **Green Bar (Happy):** The price started lower and ended higher[cite: 1, 2]. The buyers won that round[cite: 1, 2]!
* **Red Bar (Sad):** The price started higher and fell down[cite: 1, 2]. The sellers won that round[cite: 1, 2]!
* **Thin Lines (Wicks):** Show the highest sky and lowest floor the price reached during that turn[cite: 1, 2].

---

## 🛡️ 4. The 4 Golden Rules

1. **Don't Bet the Whole Piggy Bank:** If you have 100 game coins, never risk losing more than 1 or 2 coins on a single try[cite: 1, 2].
2. **Always Use Your Safety Net:** Never buy a card without setting your stop-loss floor first[cite: 1, 2].
3. **Aim for Bigger Treats:** Only make a trade if the prize you can win is at least double what you might lose (a 1-to-2 deal)[cite: 1, 2].
4. **Keep Cool:** If you lose a round, don't get mad and mash buttons to win it back[cite: 1, 2]. Take a quick break and wait for a clear chance[cite: 1, 2].
