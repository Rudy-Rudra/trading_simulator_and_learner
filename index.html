🎮 Trading Simulator Cheat Sheet (Kid-Friendly & Pro Edition)

A quick guide to trading toy cards, reading mood bars, and saving your ₹1 Lakh game coins!

🧸 1. The Game Words

  • Card (Stock): A special piece of a big company you buy (like Reliance Tech or Pixel Games).
  • Market: The playground where everyone meets to trade cards and coins.
  • Bull 🐂: A happy player who thinks prices will go up like a flying balloon!
  • Bear 🐻: A grumpy player who thinks prices will fall down like a heavy rock!
  • The Spread: The tiny difference between what buyers want to pay and what sellers want to get.

🕹️ 2. The 4 Action Buttons

Button What It Does Example
🛒 Buy Now
(Market Order)		 Grabs the card immediately, whatever it costs right now. "I want that shiny card right this second!"
⏳ Wait for My Price
(Limit Order)		 Buys automatically only if the seller drops the price to your lucky number. "I will only pay ₹500, not ₹550."
🛡️ Safety Net
(Stop-Loss)		 Sells automatically if the price starts dropping so you don't lose all your coins. "If the price falls to ₹450, sell it so I keep the rest of my coins safe."
🎁 Pocket the Prize
(Take-Profit)		 Sells automatically once you reach your target reward. "If it hits ₹650, sell it and keep the prize!"

🕯️ 3. Candlesticks (The Mood Bars)

  • 🟢 Green Bar (Happy): The price started lower and ended higher! The buyers won that round.
  • 🔴 Red Bar (Sad): The price started higher and fell down. The sellers won that round.
  • Thin Lines (Wicks): Show the highest sky and lowest floor the price reached during that turn.

🛡️ 4. The 4 Golden Rules

  1. Don't Bet the Whole Piggy Bank: If you have ₹1 Lakh, never risk losing more than 1% to 2% on a single try.
  2. Always Use Your Safety Net: Never buy a card without setting your stop-loss floor first!
  3. Aim for Bigger Treats: Only make a trade if the prize you can win is at least double what you might lose (a 1-to-2 deal).
  4. Keep Cool: If you lose a round, don't get mad and mash buttons to win it back. Take a quick break and wait for a clear chance!
